Sujit Malakar aka Babu Bhaiya
Office Staff · Academic Block
24
Hero - The Bureaucratic Liaison Sujit Malakar (Babu Da) is an office staff from Behala working in the MBAEx Office, having previously served as a Projector Operator. From his perspective, everyone on campus is simply a student. Observing changes over the last few years, he notes that while a strong bonding existed earlier, recent batches are not very into talking, likely due to an increased time crunch and pressure. He observes that some individuals interact from term 1 to term 4, but subsequently get busy with exams and placements, thereby reducing the interactions P.S. Next time you see him, ask him out for lunch to make his day.

Rajarshi Duttagupta When I first approached Ramesh at the gate to request an interview, he looked surprised — almost suspicious. "Why would anyone want to know my story?" That question stayed with me throughout this project. We are trained as managers to focus on strategy, structure, systems. But this assignment forced me to confront what we so often ignore: the human infrastructure beneath every institution.

Sachin Kumar Bijoy bhaiya showed me the electrical panel room that sits right beneath the main auditorium. Hundreds of circuits, all labelled in his handwriting. "No one else knows this map," he said. I realised then that entire systems depend on the memory of single individuals — and what a fragile, extraordinary thing that is. This is not just a people story. It is a risk management story too.

Rahul Sadhwani What moved me most was Sunita's dignity. She works in invisibility by design — early hours, quiet corridors — and has made peace with it. But the moment I sat down with her and asked her story, something in her face changed. She sat straighter. She spoke longer. Being seen, it turns out, is a profound human need. As future managers, we would do well to remember that.
